# Options Trading Mastery Course

**Duration**: 30 lectures over 10-12 weeks | **Level**: Beginner → Professional | **Prerequisite**: Basic stock market knowledge

---

## Module 1: Foundations of Options (Lectures 1-3)

### Lecture 1: What is an Option? (Calls, Puts & Basics)
- Definition and contract mechanics
- Calls vs Puts: rights vs obligations
- Intrinsic value and time value
- Why options exist (hedging, leverage, income)
- Real example: Nifty 50 call options on NSE

### Lecture 2: How Options Work (Strike, Expiry, Settlement)
- Strike price and moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM)
- Expiration dates and their significance
- European vs American options
- Settlement: cash vs physical delivery in India
- Nifty options ecosystem (weekly, monthly expiries)

### Lecture 3: Option Payoffs & Risk Diagrams
- Payoff diagrams: long call, long put, short call, short put
- Max profit and max loss concepts
- Break-even points
- Greeks overview (delta, gamma, theta preview)
- Why payoff matters for strategy selection

## Module 2: Core Concepts (Lectures 4-6)

### Lecture 4: Moneyness, Volatility & Time Value
- ITM/ATM/OTM definitions and pricing implications
- Implied volatility (IV): what it means, how it affects price
- Historical volatility vs implied volatility
- Volatility skew in real markets
- VIX as fear gauge (India equivalent: INDIAVIX)

### Lecture 5: Time Decay and Theta
- Time decay mechanics: why options lose value
- Theta acceleration: decay accelerates near expiry
- Theta's relationship to moneyness
- Practical impact: holding calls/puts to expiry
- Using decay: theta strategies overview

### Lecture 6: Market Microstructure for Options
- Bid-ask spreads and liquidity
- Open interest and trading volume
- Volume-weighted average price (VWAP)
- Liquidity considerations for entry/exit
- NSE options: liquid strikes vs illiquid strikes

## Module 3: The Greeks (Lectures 7-10)

### Lecture 7: Delta – Directional Sensitivity
- Delta definition: % price change per underlying move
- Range: -1 to +1, interpretation
- Delta for calls vs puts
- Delta changes with moneyness
- Practical use: hedge ratio calculation
- Nifty example: Nifty 18000 call vs 19000 call delta

### Lecture 8: Gamma – Delta's Rate of Change
- Gamma definition: how delta changes
- Gamma risk: why it matters in fast markets
- Gamma explosion near expiry
- Gamma vs delta trade-off in strategy selection
- Real scenario: gap opening in Nifty due to news

### Lecture 9: Theta – Time Decay Impact
- Theta recap: decay per day
- Theta's curve: accelerates 2-3 weeks before expiry
- Theta decay accelerates with OTM options
- Using theta in income strategies
- Balancing long theta (short calls) vs short theta (long calls)

### Lecture 10: Vega & Rho – Volatility & Interest Rates
- Vega: sensitivity to volatility changes
- When vega matters most (long options near ATM)
- Vega scalping: trading volatility
- Rho: interest rate sensitivity (minor impact in equity options)
- Putting Greeks together: portfolio Greeks

## Module 5: Basic Strategies (Lectures 14-17)

### Lecture 14: Directional Bets – Long Calls & Long Puts
- Long call: mechanics, risk/reward, when to use
- Long put: mechanics, risk/reward, when to use
- Choosing strike: ATM (delta ~50), OTM (delta ~20-30)
- Managing time decay for long options
- Entry/exit rules for directional trades
- Example: Bank Nifty long call setup

### Lecture 15: Spreads Part 1 – Bull Call & Bear Call
- Bull call spread: long call + short call (higher strike)
- Mechanics: reduced cost, capped profit
- Bear call spread: short call + long call (higher strike)
- Use cases: directional with defined risk
- Break-even and profit targets
- Risk management: managing short call assignment

### Lecture 16: Spreads Part 2 – Straddles & Strangles
- Long straddle: long call + long put (same strike)
- Long strangle: long call + long put (different strikes)
- Use case: expecting big move, unsure direction
- IV crush: how high IV at entry hurts exit
- Managing position as underlying moves
- Example: Nifty earnings straddle

### Lecture 17: Writing Strategies – Covered Calls & Cash Puts
- Covered call: long stock + short call
- Purpose: income generation on held stock
- Trade-off: upside cap for income
- Cash-secured put: selling puts with cash set aside
- Assignment management and follow-up
- Tax implications in India

## Module 6: Risk Management (Lectures 18-20)

### Lecture 18: Position Sizing & Kelly Criterion
- Position size based on portfolio risk tolerance
- 1% risk rule: max loss per trade = 1% of portfolio
- Kelly criterion: optimal position sizing with historical edge
- Practical implementation: from 0.5% to 2% risk per trade
- Scaling: how to size larger positions as capital grows

### Lecture 19: Stop Losses, Profit Targets & Portfolio Greeks
- Hard stops: percentage-based (stop at 25% loss)
- Soft stops: technical levels (support/resistance)
- Time-based stops: exiting if thesis doesn't play out
- Profit target rules: taking partial profits
- Portfolio delta, gamma, theta: managing at portfolio level
- Rebalancing when Greeks drift

### Lecture 20: Tail Risk, Gap Risk & Assignment Risk
- Tail events: managing outsized moves (earnings, policy changes)
- Gap risk: overnight gaps that skip stops
- Assignment risk: early assignment on short options
- Dividend dates and assignment
- Insurance strategies: buying protective puts for portfolios
- Scenario analysis: stress testing positions

### Lecture 23: Event-Driven Strategies

### Lecture 24: Volatility Strategies & Mean Reversion

- **Mindset**: Treat it as a business, not gambling; have an edge before risking real money
- **Knowledge**: Understand why each strategy works, its risks, and when NOT to use it
- **Psychology**: Execute mechanical systems; avoid emotional decisions
- **Real-world ready**: Move from paper to small real-money account with confidence
